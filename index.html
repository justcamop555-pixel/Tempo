Start-Menu shortcut · listed in Settings › Apps · clean uninstall

FAQ

Common questions

Is Tempo really free?

Yes — completely free and open, no account, no ads, no telemetry. It runs entirely on your PC.

Why does Windows say "Unknown publisher"?

Tempo isn't code-signed (certificates are costly for a free app), so SmartScreen shows a caution the first time. Click More info → Run anyway. You can verify your build with certutil -hashfile Tempo.exe SHA256 against the published checksum.

Will it get me banned from a game?

Possibly. Auto-clicking can violate a game's terms of service, and some anti-cheat systems detect it. Use it responsibly — you're responsible for how you use it. Reasonable speeds and the randomization options soften the obvious-robot pattern, but nothing makes it undetectable.

What's a safe clicking speed?

For most uses the low hundreds of CPS is plenty. Very high speeds can trip anti-cheat and stress your PC. Tempo's anti-freeze protection is on by default — leave it on. The CPS presets (10 / 50 / 100 / 200) are good starting points, and the active one is highlighted.

Where are my settings stored?

In %LOCALAPPDATA%\AutoClicker. That folder survives updates and can be backed up or deleted. There's no cloud copy.

Does it slow down or freeze my PC at high speeds?

Tempo has built-in anti-freeze protection: a CPS cap plus a CPU-adaptive throttle that automatically eases off if your system gets busy, so the machine stays responsive. It's on by default.

What languages does it support?

Six: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese. Tempo matches your Windows display language on first run, and you can change it any time in Settings (it applies after a quick restart).

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

No — Tempo is a Windows app (64-bit Windows 10 or 11). The .NET runtime is built in, so there's nothing else to install.
