## Tempo 1.0.221

**Properly fixes the scroll corruption at its root - controls no longer break after scrolling, and no crash**
- The real cause was the rendering approach for the scrollable tab pages: they were double-buffered, which only buffers the page's own painting and NOT its child controls. When the page scrolled, Windows blitted the checkboxes and labels to a new offset and only repainted a thin strip, leaving them hollow and clipped - the corruption you kept seeing.
- Switched to the correct technique: WS_EX_COMPOSITED, which composites the whole page AND every control together in one pass. Children are always drawn in the right place as part of the composite, so scrolling no longer corrupts them - and it removes the fragile repaint logic that caused the 1.0.219/1.0.220 crash, so there's no recursion to crash on.
- The animated-backdrop repaint logic was simplified accordingly (the backdrop is off by default, so there's no added delay for most setups).

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed - click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
