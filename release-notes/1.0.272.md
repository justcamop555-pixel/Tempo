## Tempo 1.0.272

**Speakers get colours · opening words come out whole · fake "[Music]" tags scrubbed**

### Every speaker gets a colour
The caption bar now colours each speaker's label so you can follow a conversation at
a glance:

- **Speaker 1** is sky blue, **Speaker 2** green, **Speaker 3** pink — nine stable,
  distinct colours in total. The same speaker always keeps the same colour.
- The **"♪ App ·" source tag** (which app the audio comes from) is now a muted grey,
  so it informs without competing with the actual words.
- Your chosen caption text colour still applies to the speech itself, and the soft
  black glow/outline still wraps every word, so readability over games and videos is
  unchanged.

Verified live against a real YouTube video: muted "♪ YouTube ·" tag, blue
"Speaker 1:", amber speech text, clean centred wrapping across lines.

### Opening words come out whole
Since 1.0.271 the audio stream never sleeps — which exposed a subtle next problem:
a transcription chunk grabbed just as someone starts talking was mostly silence with
only the first syllable at its very end, so the opening word could come out mangled.
The engine now:

- **Trims the silent lead-in** from the chunk (keeping a short natural ramp), and
- **Waits a beat when speech has only just begun**, so the first word reaches the
  speech engine complete instead of clipped.

### Whisper's imaginary sounds are scrubbed
Tempo's own caption engine (Whisper) sometimes emits things nobody said:

- Non-speech tags like **"[Music]", "(applause)", "[BLANK_AUDIO]", "♪♪"** — these are
  now stripped wherever they appear.
- Hallucinated phrases invented out of near-silence — the infamous **"Thanks for
  watching!"** and friends — are now dropped when the audio really was near-silent.
  If someone genuinely says those words at normal volume, they still caption fine.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
