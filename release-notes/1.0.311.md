## Tempo 1.0.311

**Captions that read like live speech — plus own-voice filtering, conversations that stay on the bar, and deep audio/engine work.**

### Live Captions
- **Word-by-word, animated.** Words appear one at a time at a comfortable reading pace (~210 wpm), each fading in gently — no more whole sentences slamming onto the bar too fast to finish reading. Under rapid content the pace tightens automatically so captions never drift behind the audio.
- **Conversations stay visible.** When the speaker changes, the previous turn stays on the bar in front of the new one — `Speaker 1: We'll go to the park.  Speaker 2: See you there!` — sliding off only as space runs out, like TV captions.
- **Clean line starts.** Old words shed at sentence boundaries (or a word boundary marked "…"), and a bug that could glue old turns' text under the wrong speaker label is fixed.
- **"It heard my own voice" — fixed (opt-in).** Turn on *Settings → Live Captions → "Ignore my own voice"* and Tempo compares the system audio's loudness envelope with your microphone's: your voice coming back through sidetone or chat monitoring is skipped instead of being captioned as a phantom speaker. The *other* person in a call is never dropped — their voice doesn't match your mic. (Opt-in because it opens a local mic monitor; envelope only, nothing recorded.)
- **Crosstalk:** when two faces visibly talk at once, the speaker label holds steady instead of flip-flopping. Face tracking now follows moving faces (velocity prediction), ignores face *images* (posters, thumbnails), and survives full head turns.

### Audio path (all modes)
- **5.1/7.1 dialogue clarity:** surround loopback is mixed dialogue-forward (centre channel weighted, LFE excluded) instead of flat-averaged under music and effects.
- Mic DC/rumble removed (~20 Hz high-pass) · 24-bit devices supported · input **clipping detected per channel** and flagged in Live debug · a device-unplug race that could corrupt the capture until captions were toggled is fixed.

### Engines
- **Hybrid-CPU tuning:** on P+E-core Intel chips the decoder uses the P-cores only — measured *faster with fewer threads* (whisper's lock-step math was being dragged to E-core pace).
- Pauses in speech no longer cost a wasted inference; stale context after long silences no longer re-surfaces old words; beam search automatically returns once a busy spell ends.

### Elsewhere
- **AI word fixer** protects ~150 real gaming/streaming words from false "repairs" ("respawn" was one edit from becoming "respond").
- **Enchanted tray menu:** live status row with a state dot, state-aware labels (Start ⟷ Stop clicking), hotkey hints, hover-fade animation, breathing header.
- **Second cursor** hardening: reliable grab & place, no stuck buttons, per-mouse Live-debug panel.
- Live debug shows far more everywhere: decode threads, context carry, reveal pace, per-face/per-voice detail, own-voice similarity, clipping.
