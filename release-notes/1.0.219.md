## Tempo 1.0.219

**Fixed Settings controls becoming corrupted (hollow checkboxes, clipped text) after scrolling or minimizing**
- After scrolling a tab - or minimizing and reopening the window - controls could render broken: checkboxes lost their checkmarks, labels were clipped on the left ("Iways on top", "art minimised"), even though the settings themselves were unchanged. The cause: when an animated-backdrop tab page scrolled, it repainted the page background but NOT the child controls, leaving them with stale, half-scrolled pixels.
- The scroll handler now forces a full repaint INCLUDING all child controls, so checkboxes, labels and inputs always redraw correctly after a scroll. The minimize/restore and alt-tab paths already did this; the scroll path was the one missing it.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed - click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
