## Tempo 1.0.271

**Audio system fixed: captions never sleep — no more "not responding" or delays when a video or a short sound starts**

### The root cause
Windows' "record what the PC is playing" audio stream — which Tempo's own caption
engine AND the speaker-voice detector both listen to — has a quirk: **the moment your
PC goes quiet, the stream stops delivering audio entirely.** When sound comes back
(a video starts, a button clicks in any app or website, a game plays an effect), the
stream first has to wake up. On many machines that wake-up delays the opening moments
— or swallows a short sound completely. That is exactly the "sometimes not responding
or delayed" behaviour.

### The fix
While captions or speaker labels are running, Tempo now keeps the audio stream awake
by playing **perfectly inaudible silence** to the speaker. Silence is just zeros — you
can't hear it, it costs effectively no CPU, and it means the audio pipeline is always
pumping. **Every sound — a video's first word, a button click, a game effect — is heard
from its very first millisecond.**

Verified live on this build:
- After 10+ seconds of total silence, a spoken phrase was captioned **verbatim from its
  first word** ("Testing 1. 2. 3. Short sounds are heard now.").
- Tempo's keep-alive shows up as an active (silent) session on the speaker while
  captions run, and disappears when they stop.
- If your audio device changes (headphones plugged in, output switched), the keep-alive
  follows it together with the existing capture auto-reconnect.
- A PC with no speaker simply runs as before — nothing breaks.

### Also improved
- **Captions auto-start about 2× faster.** The video/game watcher now checks every
  second, so auto-start kicks in roughly 2 seconds after sound begins (was ~4–5 s).
  The safeguard that keeps a stray notification ping from triggering captions is
  unchanged.
- **Honest "no audio" status.** The caption bar now distinguishes "everything is
  healthy but nothing is playing" from "no audio is arriving at all" and says so,
  instead of sitting on "Listening…" forever. This also fixed that message never
  appearing when a microphone was selected but silent.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
