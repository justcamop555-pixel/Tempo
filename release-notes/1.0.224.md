## Tempo 1.0.224

**Fixes settings/checkboxes that didn't stick and two features that fought each other**
- Settings-tab checkboxes are no longer lost on close. Toggles like "Hide window to tray when clicking", "Confirm before exit", "Record session history", "Always on top" etc. were only written to disk when you pressed **Save Settings**; closing the window saved the old values back, silently discarding your change. Tempo now captures the Settings-tab controls on exit, so a toggle sticks whether or not you press Save.
- The Macros tab "Capture mouse movement" and "Capture keyboard" checkboxes are now remembered between launches. They had no backing setting at all, so they reset to ON every time you reopened Tempo — a keyboard-only or mouse-only recording preference could never stick. They now save the moment you toggle them.
- "Unlock max speed (advanced)" actually goes fast now. The Anti-Freeze cap (default 200 CPS) was silently clamping the unlocked rate, so unlocking and dragging the slider to 1000–2000 CPS did nothing unless you also raised the Anti-Freeze "Max CPS" by hand. Unlocking now lifts the cap automatically (and restores the safe 200 default when you re-lock); the adaptive CPU back-off still protects your system.
- "Colorful cursor trail" and the macro capture checkboxes now refresh correctly after **Reset to defaults** and **Import settings…**, instead of showing their old state until clicked.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed - click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
