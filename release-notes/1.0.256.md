## Tempo 1.0.256

**Live Captions: voices are now matched by AI-style profiling, and captions start
themselves when you watch or play something**

### Speaker labels now recognise returning voices
In 1.0.255 the labels could only count turns upward. Now Tempo listens to the voice
itself: it measures each speaker's pitch and tone on your PC (nothing is uploaded, no
model to download, and the CPU cost is negligible) and matches new speech against the
voices it has heard so far. When the first person talks again, the label goes back to
**Speaker 1** — verified with two different Windows voices:

```
Speaker 1: Hello everyone, this is the host speaking to you.   (male voice)
Speaker 2: Thank you so much. I am really excited to be here.  (female voice)
Speaker 1: Great to hear that. Let us get started.             (male voice again → 1!)
```

A changed voice can also split a turn after only a half-second pause, so quick
back-and-forth conversation labels correctly. Honest limits: two very similar voices can
still be confused, and if audio capture isn't possible the labels fall back to the
pause-based turn counting from 1.0.255.

### Captions auto-start for videos & games
Tempo now notices when you're watching or playing something with sound and turns
captions on automatically:

- **Video sites in any browser** — detected from the window title: YouTube, TikTok,
  Twitch, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, Crunchyroll, Vimeo and more.
- **Games** — detected by process: Roblox, Call of Duty / Warzone, Rainbow Six,
  Fortnite, Valorant, CS2, Apex Legends, PUBG, Overwatch, Minecraft, GTA V and more.
- Only when **audio is actually playing** — a muted tab or an idle homepage does nothing.
- **It never fights you:** turn captions off during a video and auto-start stays quiet
  until that video or game is over.

On by default; toggle it in **Settings → Live Captions → "Auto-start for videos & games"**.

### Small fix
Long monologues no longer push the "Speaker N:" label off the left edge of the caption
bar — the label sticks and the newest words follow an ellipsis.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
