## Tempo 1.0.310

**A hardening release: the Second Cursor's real-use bugs fixed, and Live Captions gets genuine accuracy fixes, multi-monitor support, and X-ray debug views.**

### Second Cursor — the bugs two-mouse testing found (still experimental)
- **Click, hold and drag now land where you aim.** A click that moved even a pixel used to press at the second cursor but release back at the parked spot — a broken click. The pointer now follows during a hold/drag and stays parked only while aiming.
- **The wheel scrolls the right window.** The second mouse's scroll used to scroll whatever sat under your *parked* pointer; it's now re-sent as a real scroll at the second cursor.
- **Your main mouse stopped being interfered with** — its clicks were eaten for up to a third of a second after any second-mouse activity. The guard is much tighter now.
- **Grab & place is reliable** — the place-click could be swallowed if the second mouse had just moved; placement now takes priority, and the second mouse is ignored while you aim.
- **No more stuck buttons** — unplugging, re-picking or switching the bound mouse mid-hold used to leave the real button held down forever. Every path now releases it.
- **The marker stays visible** above apps that assert always-on-top after it, and settings tweaks no longer re-bind the mouse over and over.
- **Live debug shows everything:** per-mouse activity (▶ = driving the second cursor, ● MOVING NOW), real-cursor HELD STILL / free with the parked coordinates, held buttons, and bind status.

The honest limits from 1.0.309 still stand: Windows has one hardware cursor, so cursor-locked mouse-look games (most FPS) can't be split, and raw-input games also read your main mouse.

### Live Captions
- **Seamless audio into the AI.** Every 40 ms audio buffer was resampled in isolation, throwing away a fraction of a sample ~25× per second — a constant, invisible degradation (worst on 44.1 kHz devices). The audio is now one continuous, seamless stream.
- **Repeated speech isn't swallowed any more.** Saying "Okay." twice a minute apart used to show it once — the seam de-duplicator treated the repeat as a chunk artifact. It now only de-dupes where chunks can physically overlap.
- **The source tag stops lying when you switch tabs.** Audio is now attributed to the *process* making it, so switching browser tabs (or windows) mid-video can't re-brand the tag — and speaker numbering no longer gets wiped by those false "source changes".
- **Captions on the monitor with the sound.** The bar used to be pinned to the primary screen; it now appears on whichever monitor holds the window making the sound (your game on the DisplayPort screen gets its captions *there*). If you drag the bar somewhere yourself, it stays put.
- **Japanese/Chinese captions keep their punctuation** (。、！？ were being stripped by the symbol filter).
- **Decode-loop stutters collapse** ("blue-hipped blue-hipped blue-hipped…"), while natural repeats ("I know, I know") are left alone.
- **Speed headroom is spent on accuracy:** fast engines carry more sentence context across chunk boundaries, and beam-search decoding automatically returns after a slowdown forced it off.
- **Gaming words are safe from the word-fixer** — "respawn" was one letter away from being "corrected" into "respond". Real gaming/streaming vocabulary is now protected.
- **Face analysis survives head turns** — a face the detector loses (turned left/right, looking down/up) keeps being tracked at its last spot with mouth motion still measured, so the speaker label outlives the turn.
- **X-ray views in Live debug:** every tracked face (position · size · mouth-motion · TALKING/COASTING) and every learned voice fingerprint (pitch · brightness · intonation · evidence · ● SPEAKING).
- **Crisper caption text** with the same fonts — cleaner rasterisation of the glow/outline/fill layers.
- Faster too: the capture path is now allocation-free in steady state.
