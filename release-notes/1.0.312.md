## Tempo 1.0.312

**Tempo notifications — animated, Windows 11-style, and clickable. Plus a caption-bar crash fix, real keep-up on mid CPUs, and sharper AI.**

### New: Tempo's own notifications
Tempo now shows notifications as its own animated cards in the screen corner you choose — styled like Windows 11, with the app's icon, a title, a description, and (for the test/own cards) a picture. They slide in with a fade, auto-dismiss after a few seconds (**hover to hold**, click ✕ to close), and stack neatly. On a multi-monitor PC they always appear together on the monitor Tempo is on. *Settings → Notifications.*

- **Mirror Windows notifications (opt-in).** Turn it on and other apps' Windows notifications show up as Tempo cards in your style — each with the **source app's real icon** (Discord's logo for Discord, and so on). It uses the on-device notification-listener API; nothing leaves your PC. Mirroring is near-instant — a fast-path event plus a 200 ms poll — so the card appears while the Windows toast is still on screen, not seconds after it's gone.
- **Click a card to open the app that sent it** — any app or browser, exactly like clicking a Windows 11 toast. Tempo's own cards open the Tempo window. The ✕ only dismisses.
- **Remembered instantly.** Every notification setting (on/off, corner, duration, mirror, clear-from-Action-Center) is saved the moment you change it — it sticks even through a crash or force-quit, with no Save needed.
- **Honest about the limits.** To stop Windows' *own* banner pop-ups so only Tempo's show, turn on Windows **Do not disturb** — a one-click button opens the right setting, and the mirror keeps working under Do not disturb. No app can suppress another app's banner, and another app's *inline* image (like a screenshot thumbnail) isn't exposed by Windows — so Tempo shows the app's icon/picture and its text.

### Live Captions
- **Crash fixed.** A caption-bar paint crash — "Parameter is not valid" on foreign-script or glyph-less words — is fixed with path guards and a protected repaint.
- **Real keep-up on mid CPUs.** After any hitch, new **catch-up decoding** drains the backlog in one oversized pass instead of trailing for seconds. Entering the "stretched" tier now also drops beam search for the headroom it needs, and a machine that quietly sheds words in the 1.0–1.4× real-time band now **escalates** (beam off, then a smaller model) instead of losing words forever. End-of-utterance early takes trim latency further.
- **AI word fixer** now scans the top few OS spelling suggestions, not just the first — catching more real mis-hears while staying just as conservative.
- **Face/mouth speaker analysis** ignores scene cuts and hard camera pans (they no longer make every face read as "talking"), and raises the talking bar for fast-moving faces so a silent walker isn't tagged as the speaker.
- **Settings layout:** the "Ignore my own voice" option no longer overlaps the model buttons.

### Live Debug
Shows much more, so silent issues surface: a caption **Keep-up** line (real-time factor + a plain verdict), catch-up and quick-take counts, the 5.1 dialogue-mix indicator, per-section resilience (one failing stat can't blank the rest), and a full **Notifications** line — style, corner, duration, on-screen/queued card counts, mirror state, instant-vs-polling, and the last mirrored app.
