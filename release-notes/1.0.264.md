## Tempo 1.0.264

**Live Captions, all-round: Windows' own hotkey now brings Tempo's bar up, and
transcripts can be saved**

### Win+Ctrl+L just works now
Until now Tempo's caption bar only appeared when you toggled captions through TEMPO
(hotkey or tray). If you pressed **Win+Ctrl+L** — Windows' own Live Captions shortcut —
you got the plain Windows bar and Tempo sat idle.

Now Tempo notices within a couple of seconds, brings its own bar up (speaker labels,
app tag, word repair and all), parks the Windows bar off-screen and takes over —
regardless of which caption engine you've chosen. Verified live: pressing the Windows
hotkey alone produced Tempo's full caption bar with zero interaction with Tempo.

The watch is a cheap window-handle check every ~2 seconds (no measurable cost), and a
cooldown after Tempo's own off-toggle means the two can never fight each other.

### Save transcripts (opt-in)
New checkbox: **Settings → Live Captions → "Save transcripts to disk"**. When captions
turn off, the whole session is written to Tempo's data folder under `transcripts\`:

```
Tempo Live Captions transcript — Saturday, July 11, 2026 4:00 PM
------------------------------------------------------------
[15:59:42]  Speaker 1: Tempo noticed that Windows Live captions were turned on by hand
[16:00:18]  ♪ Powershell · Speaker 1: The watcher saw the Windows captions and switched
            the Tempo bar on all by itself.
```

One file per session, every line timestamped, speaker labels and "♪ app" tags intact.
**Off by default** — it writes spoken content to disk — and everything stays on your PC.

### Session hygiene
Each caption session now starts with a fresh history: the "Show full history" panel no
longer carries lines over from hours ago, and history lines carry real timestamps.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
