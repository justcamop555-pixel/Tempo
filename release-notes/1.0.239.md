## Tempo 1.0.239

**Tabs now remember where you scrolled**

Each tab keeps its own scroll position. Scroll down on the Clicker (or Macros, or
Statistics) tab, switch to another tab, come back — and you're right where you left
off, instead of being thrown back to the top.

- Every tab's up/down scroll position is remembered as you leave it and restored when
  you return.
- Fixed the underlying bug that made the content snap back to the top: when the window
  re-centred its content it was repositioning controls in the wrong (scrolled)
  coordinate space, which reset the scroll. It now repositions in unscrolled space and
  re-applies your exact scroll afterwards — so the position actually sticks.

Cumulative — includes the blank-screen-on-restore fix (1.0.238), the full visual
redesign (1.0.237) and everything before it.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
