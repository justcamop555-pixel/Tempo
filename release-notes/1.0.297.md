## Tempo 1.0.297

**Four Settings features levelled up: the start-delay, the update check, the running overlay, and startup launch**

### The start-delay countdown is no longer a blind, forced wait

- It now shows **what it's about to start** — e.g. "Starting: Left · 12 CPS" — right under the number.
- You can **abort it**: press Esc *or* your stop hotkey and the count-in cancels, instead of making you sit through a run you've changed your mind about.
- An optional **per-second beep** (with a higher note on GO) gives an audible count-in — tick the "Beep" box next to the start delay.

### Update checking gained a frequency and an inline status

Instead of a bare on/off toggle, you now choose how often the automatic check runs:
**Every launch / Daily / Weekly**.

And the "last checked" line tells you the result at a glance — **"You're up to date"** or
**"Update available: vX"** — so you don't have to open the update dialog to know where you stand.

### The running overlay is now customisable

A **"Customise…"** button (next to "Show on-screen overlay while running") opens a small
dialog where you can set:

- its **screen corner** — top/bottom, left/centre/right (six positions);
- its **opacity**, 40–100%;
- exactly what it shows — **click count**, **CPS**, and/or **elapsed time**.

Changes apply live while the badge is on screen.

### Startup launch got a delay option

When Windows launches Tempo at sign-in, an optional **Startup delay** (in the Startup &
Window card) holds its background update check back by a few seconds, so it doesn't
compete with everything else booting for the network. It has no effect when you open
Tempo yourself. (Tempo already self-heals a startup entry a cleaner removed, and warns
you if Windows blocked it.)
