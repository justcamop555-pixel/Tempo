## Tempo 1.0.269

**First words appear much sooner — and the engine now guarantees it keeps up**

### Fast start: less delay when someone starts talking
Tempo's own engine used to wait for a full 2.2-second slice of audio before its very
first transcription pass, so words showed up roughly 3 seconds after someone started
speaking. Now, whenever captions are idle — a fresh session, a silence, a new speaker
after a lull — the engine transcribes a much shorter first slice, putting the opening
words on screen in about **2 to 2.8 seconds**. Once text is flowing, it returns to full
windows, because longer windows hear better mid-stream.

### The engine refuses to fall behind (automatic model downgrade)
A speech model that needs longer to transcribe a chunk than the chunk lasts can *never*
catch up — captions drift further behind forever. That's exactly what happened with
**Large Turbo** on a typical CPU: measured at ~36 seconds per chunk, captions arrived
half a minute late and even started hallucinating under pressure.

Tempo now times every chunk. When the chosen model repeatedly can't keep up:

1. it automatically switches to the next smaller model you have installed —
   Large → Medium → Small → Base → Tiny — **for this session only**;
2. your saved model choice is never changed;
3. a notification tells you what happened and why.

Verified live: Large Turbo was lagging ~37 seconds behind; Tempo switched itself to Base
and the very next sentence appeared in **2.8 seconds**, cleanly transcribed.

If you want Large Turbo's accuracy, it remains ideal for machines with strong GPUs/CPUs —
and Tempo will now tell you honestly when your machine isn't keeping up instead of
silently lagging.

### Honest correction from 1.0.268
The "beam search" decoding added last release has been switched off. Its speed
measurement was confounded — the test machine had quietly switched to Large Turbo, which
alone explains the slowdown — but greedy decoding is the proven-safe choice for
real-time captions, so beam stays off (kept behind a flag for a future engine upgrade).
