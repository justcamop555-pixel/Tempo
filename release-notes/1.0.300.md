## Tempo 1.0.300

**Two apps playing at once no longer wreck the speakers · Live debug stops yanking you while you read**

### Dual audio no longer resets speakers every second
A real Live-debug export showed the problem exactly: with a game (Call of Duty) AND
a YouTube video both audible, the "♪ who is playing" tag flapped between them — and
**every flap forgot the learned voices and restarted the speaker numbering**, so
labels never had a chance to settle.

Fixed in two layers:

- **The tag switches only on a genuine hand-over.** A challenger now has to win the
  loudness race NET of the incumbent's wins — two apps trading the lead back and
  forth hovers at zero and never switches, while a real change (the incumbent went
  quiet) still switches within ~5 seconds.
- **Voice resets are rate-limited.** Even when the tag does legitimately change, the
  "forget voices + restart numbering" reset now fires at most once per 20 seconds —
  during mixed audio the voices that were already learned survive.

Verified live: two programs deliberately talking over each other for 20 seconds
produced **zero** tag changes and **zero** resets. The same condition previously
logged a reset every second or two.

### Live debug respects your scrolling
Scrolling up to read older events used to get yanked back to the newest line the
moment anything new was logged. The view now auto-follows **only when you're already
at the tail** — scrolled up, new lines append quietly below and your reading
position stays exactly where you put it. Verified live.

### Housekeeping
The GPU-captions option that an older build (1.0.290) permanently switched off
during a gaming session has been restored — the session-only GPU fallback shipped
since then makes it safe to leave ON: a busy GPU costs one session, not the setting.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
