## Tempo 1.0.207

**Tempo can now caption on its own; the empty caption box on startup is gone; the caption bar matches your theme**
- Caption source now has an Auto mode (the new default): Tempo transcribes your PC's audio itself, offline, and auto-falls-back to mirroring Windows 11 Live Captions if its own engine can't run (no speech model, missing engine files, or no audio device) - so you always get captions, fully offline when possible. Pick it under Settings > Captions.
- No empty box on startup: the bar no longer pops a full empty panel with placeholder text while the engine spins up. Real text replaces the starting state the moment it arrives. Applies to both Tempo's own engine and the Windows mirror.
- Caption bar now matches your theme: the panel and border colors come from the active theme instead of a hardcoded near-black. No new theme - existing themes only.
- Auto-fallback fires at most once per session and never overwrites your chosen source, so turning captions on next time tries the offline engine again.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed - click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
