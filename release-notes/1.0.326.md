### 1.0.326
**Roblox sign-in that saves itself · pick a Roblox version on your PC · Tempo's own scroll bars · captions that change language live · a Dim slider that keeps up · more in Live debug**

#### Roblox and accounts
- **Sign-in saves itself.** Press Log in to Roblox, log in or sign up in the private browser window, and Tempo saves the account and closes the browser. The small "Log in to Roblox" window and the account screen after it are gone. The line under the account list says what is happening, and the button reads **Cancel sign-in** while the browser is open.
- **No duplicate accounts.** Signing in to an account you already had added it again; now its session is refreshed. A re-login as a different account than the one you chose goes to that account, and the one you chose is left as it was.
- **Your password is saved when you create an account too,** not only when you log in. A passkey or "quick log in" still saves only the username.
- **Pick a Roblox version.** A new "Roblox version" row lists Automatic and every Roblox client already on your PC (Roblox, Bloxstrap, Fishstrap, Voidstrap). Nothing is downloaded, and only copies signed by Roblox are started. An older version gets a warning, since Roblox may update or refuse it. If a Roblox update deletes the one you picked, Tempo goes back to Automatic and says so.
- **Fixed:**
  - The local API could open a second sign-in browser over an open one.
  - Renaming an account while signing it in again lost the new session.
  - Turning the account manager off, resetting the vault or closing Tempo left the sign-in browser open.

#### Tempo's own scroll bars
- **Slim, themed scroll bars with up and down arrows** replace Windows 11's everywhere: the pages, lists, text boxes, Live debug, dialogs and drop-down lists. They widen when you point at them and take the accent while you drag. On long lists the thumb never shrinks to a dot.
- **Scrolling is unchanged.** Windows' own scroll bar still does every drag, click, wheel turn and key underneath. Only the look is new.

#### Live Captions
- **Change the caption language while captions run.** It applies at once, without a restart, and the transcript is kept.
- **Speaker names stay** when "Use faces too" is turned on or off. They were all reset to Speaker 1.
- **Long sessions save from the start.** A saved transcript held only the last 500 lines.
- **The caption bar speaks your language,** a line said twice isn't dropped, a selection in the transcript stays put, and the transcript grows with the window.

#### Look and the rest
- **The Dim slider responds at once.** Each step reloaded the background picture. It's greyed out with no picture, and on a light theme it tones the picture towards the theme.
- **Steadier pages.** The tab-switch picture always lifts and a paint freeze ends with a full repaint, after pages were seen in pieces on a 125% laptop.
- **Live debug:**
  - New lines that arrive below where you're reading are counted beside the filter. Click the count to jump to them.
  - A "Sign-in:" line shows whether a sign-in browser is open, for how long and whether a password was typed (never the password), or how the last one ended.
  - The stats no longer snap back while you drag their scroll bar.
