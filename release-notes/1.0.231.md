## Tempo 1.0.231

**New scroll bars and title bar that match the theme — and no more overlapping checkboxes**
- **Redesigned scroll bars.** The up/down scroll bars are now thin and theme-aware — dark and slim on dark themes, light on light themes — instead of the chunky light-grey Windows default. The window title bar matches too (dark in dark themes).
- **Fixed the overlapping checkboxes** in Settings → Behaviour (the ones you circled). The flat checkboxes were slightly taller than the native ones the layout was built around, so the bottom rows collided and one ran past the card edge. The checkbox height now matches the original, the rows are evenly spaced, and "Sleep in tray" was moved into the right column so the whole card fits cleanly.

Cumulative — includes the 1.0.230 scroll-ghosting fix, the 1.0.229 wallpaper transparency, the 1.0.228 redesign, and the 1.0.227 smooth-scroll fix.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed - click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
