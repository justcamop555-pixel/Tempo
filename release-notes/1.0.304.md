## Tempo 1.0.304

**Captions no longer cost FPS in games · wrong-tab fix · Live debug sees everything · visual polish**

### Play with captions on — without losing frames
Captions during games were measurably dropping FPS, for three stacked reasons: the
GPU speech engine's inference bursts (made MORE frequent by its earned fast pace),
the face analyser capturing the game window several times a second, and the caption
bar's ~30 fps glow animation.

Tempo now notices a fullscreen app taking the screen and automatically switches to
**low-impact game mode**: relaxed engine pace with beam search off, 1 fps face
analysis, and a calmer overlay. Captions keep working the whole time — a beat
slower, in exchange for your frame rate. The moment the game closes, everything
restores itself, beam search included. Live debug shows "Game mode: 🎮" while it's
active, and the Health panel explains it in plain English.

### The wrong-tab fix
Sitting on a Google tab while audio played elsewhere in Edge used to credit
"♪ Google" on the caption bar. Now, when the audible app is a browser whose active
tab isn't a recognised media site, Tempo scans the browser's other windows for one
that IS (YouTube, Spotify, Twitch…) before falling back to the active tab.

### Live debug: nothing hidden
- Devices marked **[chosen]** or **[default]** in the stats.
- Game mode visible in stats AND explained in Health.
- Warnings when your chosen speaker/microphone is unplugged.
- The microphone/speaker Yes-No prompts now log when shown, accepted, or declined.
- **True colours**: a saturated palette variant for light themes (the pale set
  washed out on white), and the "system" category moved off pale-cyan so it can't
  be confused with captions' sky blue.

### Visual polish
- **The tray menu is Tempo-themed now** — dark surface, rounded accent highlight,
  themed separators and check marks, and a glyph per item — instead of the stark
  white system menu. It follows Dark/Light/Match Windows live.
- **Every sidebar tab got its own icon colour** — Clicker orange, Multi-Point mint,
  Macros lavender, Statistics sky blue, Keybinds gold, Settings orchid — so the
  tabs read at a glance. Light themes get darker variants automatically.

(This release also includes everything tested since 1.0.303: the speaker/microphone
device pickers with honest model detection remain as shipped.)

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
