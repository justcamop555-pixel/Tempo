## Tempo 1.0.275

**Tempo now detects whether your PC has a speaker — by name, live, with auto-recovery**

### See exactly what Tempo can hear
Tempo's captions work by listening to "what the PC is playing" through your speaker.
This release makes that visible and self-healing:

- **A live device line in Settings → Live Captions** shows the speaker Tempo sees
  right now, by name — for example:

  > 🔊 Speaker detected: KJW24FVX (NVIDIA High Definition Audio) · 🎙 microphone ready

  It refreshes every few seconds as devices come and go (headphones, Bluetooth,
  USB, HDMI). If your PC has **no** speaker, it turns into a clear warning —
  "⚠ No speaker found — Tempo can't hear system audio" — and tells you what to do
  (switch "Listen to" to Microphone, or connect a device).

- **Captions name their device when they start.** The status line now reads e.g.
  "Tempo Live Captions started · system audio · KJW24FVX (NVIDIA High Definition
  Audio)" — so you can tell at a glance whether Tempo is listening to the right
  output (the one your game or video is actually playing through).

### Captions auto-recover when a speaker appears
Previously, if captions were started with no speaker — or the audio device
disappeared mid-session and couldn't be reopened — the caption engine stayed dead
until you toggled Live Captions off and on. Now Tempo *watches* for a speaker
appearing or returning (plugging in headphones, Bluetooth reconnecting, a monitor
waking up), re-points its audio keep-alive at the new device, and restarts the
caption engine automatically. No toggling.

All three parts were verified live on a real machine, including the actual device
names shown above.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
