## Tempo 1.0.257

**Live Captions: auto-start for ANY app or site, sturdier speaker matching, and a ♪ sound indicator**

### Auto-start now works everywhere — no list needed
Instead of matching against a list of famous apps, Tempo now asks Windows which
processes are actually **making sound** (the same per-app data you see in the Volume
Mixer) and checks whether the app in front is one of them:

- **Any game** — if it's in the foreground and making sound, it counts. Roblox, COD and
  the other big names still show their proper name; an unknown indie game is identified
  by its window title.
- **Any video site in any browser** — browsers play audio from helper processes, and
  Tempo matches those correctly. YouTube/TikTok/Twitch/Netflix show their proper names;
  an unknown site shows its page title.
- **No false alarms** — a notification ping or a click sound can't trigger it: the app
  must be audibly playing on two consecutive checks (~4 seconds).

Verified live: a media player playing a file in the foreground auto-started captions
with no interaction at all.

### Fixed: the same person kept becoming "Speaker 2" or "Speaker 3"
Three real defects in the voice matcher, all fixed and re-verified:

1. **Octave errors** — the pitch detector could read the same voice an octave off, which
   looked like a brand-new speaker. It now picks the octave-safe peak.
2. **Sentence-dependent brightness** — voice "brightness" was measured across all sound,
   so a hissy, s-heavy sentence looked like a different person. It's now measured only
   on vowel frames, which is a property of the voice, not the words.
3. **The verdict arrived late** — a returning speaker's first words often hit the screen
   before the voice analysis had heard enough to name them, so the turn kept the previous
   number. While a turn is young, the verdict now corrects the label in place.

Re-verified with two different voices: 1 → 2 → 1 → (music) → 2, all correct.

### ♪ The captions now tell you about non-speech sound
When audio keeps playing but nobody has spoken for ~18 seconds — music, game sound
effects, ambience — the bar now shows **"♪ Music or sounds playing — no speech"**
instead of a stale old sentence. The moment someone speaks, real captions return.

One honest note: identifying people/objects **visually** (who's on screen, what's
happening in the picture) needs computer-vision AI far beyond what runs quietly in an
auto-clicker — Tempo describes what it can *hear*: who's talking (by voice), what's
being said, and whether non-speech sound is playing.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
