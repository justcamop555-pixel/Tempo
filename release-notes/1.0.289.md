## Tempo 1.0.289

**Speaker fix: one narrator no longer becomes "Speaker 2 / 3" mid-video**

### The bug you reported
Watching a YouTube video with a single narrator: the captions started with
**Speaker 1**, then flipped to **Speaker 2** (and 3) while the very same person kept
talking.

### What was happening
Two things stacked up:

1. Any silent pause longer than ~4 seconds (a music break, a scene change, a slow
   moment) made the old pause-heuristic **count up** the speaker number whenever the
   voice verdict wasn't ready at that exact instant.
2. Background music mixed underneath the voice could shift the measured voice
   features long enough to mint a **phantom voice profile** — which then "confirmed"
   the wrong new number.

### The fix — two layers
- **Pauses don't rename people anymore.** While the voice detector is running, a
  pause alone no longer changes the speaker number — the VOICE is what changes it.
  If a new person really does start talking, the voice verdict lands within about a
  second and the label corrects itself in place, exactly as before. (On PCs where
  voice detection can't run, the old pause behaviour remains — there it's the only
  signal available.)
- **Phantom voices got harder to mint.** The detector now needs ~50% longer of
  consistently non-matching audio (~1 second) before inventing a brand-new speaker
  profile, so music under one narrator's voice can't fake a second person — while
  real speaker changes still classify quickly.

### Verified on the real thing
Tested against an actual YouTube video with one narrator, natural pauses and a music
break: the label held **Speaker 1** throughout. Real voice changes still switch
labels normally.
