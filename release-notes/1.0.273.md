## Tempo 1.0.273

**Speech engine core upgraded — captions hear noticeably better on every model**

### The engine under the captions is now two years newer
Tempo's own Live Captions run on the Whisper speech engine. This release upgrades its
runtime from an early-2024 build to the current one — bringing roughly two years of
upstream recognition, decoding and stability fixes to **every** model, tiny through
Large Turbo, with no change needed to your installed model files.

Verified live on this build: the *small* "base" model transcribed a test sentence
near-verbatim ("Fox jumps over the lazy dog while the engine listens carefully to
every word.") while keeping real-time pace.

### Beam-search hearing for the light models
On a 6+ core CPU, the tiny/base/small models now decode with **beam search** — the
engine weighs several possible word paths instead of committing to its first guess,
which audibly reduces wrong words. This was designed in long ago but the old runtime
had a bug that made it ~30 seconds per chunk; the new runtime runs it at full speed
(re-measured on this machine — captions keep pace). Medium and Large stay on the fast
decoder to protect real-time pacing, and the automatic model-downgrade watchdog still
protects slower PCs.

### Three new accuracy filters
- **Phantom-word gate** — the engine reads Whisper's own confidence per segment and
  drops one-to-three-word segments the model itself doubted: the classic "background
  noise heard as a word". Longer low-confidence speech still shows — real mumbling is
  better shown than swallowed. Verified live that real short phrases ("Danger ahead,
  turn left now.") come through intact from the first word.
- **Smarter seam de-duplication** — listening windows overlap, and Whisper sometimes
  re-hears the seam with different word breaks ("keep alive" → "keep a live"). The
  duplicate detector now also compares the seam letter-by-letter, so re-segmented
  repeats vanish instead of stuttering the caption.
- **Decode-loop stutter collapse** — on noisy audio Whisper occasionally repeats the
  same phrase back-to-back ("and the bird flies and the bird flies"). Immediate
  repeats of 3+ word phrases now collapse to one copy; short natural repeats like
  "I know, I know" are deliberately left alone.
