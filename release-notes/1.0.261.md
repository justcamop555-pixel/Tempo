## Tempo 1.0.261

**An honest heads-up about speaker labels — plus cleaner matching and a Windows-mirror fix**

### Speaker labels now introduce themselves honestly
The first time captions start with speaker labels enabled, Tempo shows a one-time notice:

> **Speaker labels are AI guesses.** They come from on-device AI listening to voice pitch
> and pauses. It makes plenty of mistakes — similar voices can get the same number, one
> person can be split into two speakers, music and cross-talk confuse it. Treat the
> numbers as a reading aid, never as identification.

It auto-closes with a countdown (like the welcome note), offers a one-click
**"Turn labels off"**, and if captions start from the tray or a hotkey mid-game, it
arrives as a quiet tray balloon instead — no dialog ever steals your focus.

### Cleaner speaker matching
- The voice matcher **no longer learns from non-speech audio**. Music has pitch too, and
  classifying it minted phantom speakers and dragged real voice profiles toward the
  soundtrack. Now it only matches while the audio actually sounds like speech.
- Creating a brand-new speaker needs nearly **twice the evidence** of matching a known
  one — a cough, laugh or sound effect can't become "Speaker 3" anymore.

### Windows Live Captions mirror: phantom-turn fix
When the Windows caption line gets long, Windows rolls it — dropping old words to make
room. That roll stalled Tempo's speech-activity clock, so the words right after it
measured a fake "pause" and were mislabelled as a brand-new speaker mid-sentence.
Rolls now correctly count as speech activity.

### Small polish
- Whisper's occasional stray symbols ("◆", box glyphs) are stripped before they reach
  the caption bar.
- Starting captions during pure music no longer shows "listening…" forever — the
  "♪ Music or sounds playing" note takes over once the audio is clearly non-speech.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
