## Tempo 1.0.259

**Tempo's own caption engine grows up: every model size, any language, clearer UI**

### The full model range
Tempo's offline captions previously offered three speech models; now you get the whole
ladder, picked to match your PC:

| Model | Size | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Tiny (fastest) | ~75 MB | Instant even on weak PCs; rough accuracy. English only. |
| Base (fast) | ~140 MB | The default — quick with fair accuracy. English only. |
| Small (balanced) | ~460 MB | Better hearing, a bit more CPU. English only. |
| Medium (accurate) | ~1.5 GB | High accuracy, needs a strong CPU/GPU. English only. |
| **Large Turbo (best, any language)** | ~1.6 GB | Best accuracy AND understands 90+ languages. |

Large Turbo is the distilled "large-v3-turbo" build — near large-v3 accuracy at several
times the speed, which is what makes "best accuracy" actually usable for LIVE captions.

### It understands any language now
The ".en" models only know English. Large Turbo is multilingual, and Tempo now tells it
to **auto-detect the spoken language** — so a Japanese stream, a Spanish video or a
German teammate all get captioned, no setting to flip.

### Clearer model UI
- The status under the picker shows the model's **real size on disk** once installed —
  "✓ Base (fast) is installed and ready · 141 MB on disk · English only." — and when it
  isn't, points you straight at the Download button with the size you'd pull.
- Every entry in the picker spells out size, speed and language support, and the list is
  generated from the model catalogue so it always matches what Tempo can actually load.
- Downloads still go through the progress dialog, land in Tempo's models folder once,
  and work fully offline after that.

Verified end-to-end: Tempo's own engine (Base) live-captioned speech with the speaker
label and the "♪ app" source tag intact.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
