## Tempo 1.0.274

**Cleaner voice fingerprints for speakers · language stays locked once detected · more hallucinations scrubbed**

### Speaker voices are measured cleanly now
The speaker detector fingerprints each voice (pitch, brightness) from a downsampled
copy of your PC's audio. That downsampling was previously done the quick way — which
mathematically folds all sound above 8 kHz (sharp S-sounds, cymbals, game effects)
back **into** the voice band as false signal. In practice the same person could
measure "brighter" in one video and "darker" in another depending on the background,
nudging the matcher toward wrong speaker numbers.

The audio is now properly filtered while downsampling, so the fingerprints reflect
the actual voice. Verified live with the standard two-voice test: first voice →
**Speaker 1** (blue), different voice → **Speaker 2** (green), first voice again →
back to **Speaker 1**.

### Language locks in once it's sure (multilingual models)
With a multilingual model (like Large Turbo), Whisper detects the spoken language on
every chunk — and a noisy or musical chunk could make it *flip*, transcribing a
stretch as the wrong language entirely. Now:

- Once several consecutive chunks agree on a language, Tempo **locks it** for the
  session — no more mid-video flips, and each chunk skips the detection cost.
- If confidence collapses for several chunks in a row (the sign the content really
  did switch language), Tempo **unlocks and re-detects** automatically.
- English-only models (base.en, medium.en) are unaffected — they were always fixed.

### Subtitle-credit hallucinations scrubbed — in 8 languages
Whisper memorised subtitle credits from its training data and "hears" them in quiet
or musical audio: "Subtitles by the Amara.org community", "ご視聴ありがとうございました",
"Sous-titres réalisés par…", "Untertitelung des ZDF…". Nobody ever *says* these on a
soundtrack, so Tempo now drops them at any volume — English, Japanese, German,
French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Chinese variants.
