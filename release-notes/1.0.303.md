## Tempo 1.0.303

**Choose which speaker and microphone captions use — listed by model**

### Two or more devices? Pick the one captions should hear
New in Settings → Live Captions: a **🔊 speaker picker** and a **🎙 microphone
picker**. If your PC has several playback devices (headset + monitor speakers) or
several inputs (webcam mic + USB mic), you can now choose exactly which one Tempo's
captions listen through:

- Every active device is listed by its **model name** — e.g. "KJW24FVX (NVIDIA High
  Definition Audio)" or "Microphone (EMEET SmartCam C60E 4K)".
- **"Default (follow Windows)"** keeps the old behaviour and is still the default.
- The lists refresh live as devices are plugged in and removed.
- The choice applies **immediately** — the caption engine, the speaker-voice
  detector and the audio keep-alive all re-point on the spot, no restart.

### Honest when detection fails
- A device whose model the driver won't report shows as **"Unknown speaker (model
  not reported)"** — it's still selectable, it just tells you the truth.
- A class with no devices at all shows **"(no device found)"**.
- If your chosen device gets unplugged, captions **fall back to the Windows
  default** and a warning line under the pickers says exactly that. Nothing fails
  silently.

Verified live end-to-end: a specific speaker was chosen from the picker, the log
recorded "speaker picker changed — re-pointing the audio stack", and speech was
captioned through the chosen device on the GPU engine.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
