## Tempo 1.0.284

**Plug in a microphone or speaker — Tempo notices and asks (Yes/No)**

### The rescue prompt
On a PC with **no speaker and no microphone**, Tempo's captions have nothing to
listen to. Now Tempo keeps watching (device refresh every 2 seconds), and the moment
you plug in a **microphone** in that situation it asks you directly:

> A microphone was just connected:
>
> 🎙 *USB Microphone (Blue Snowball)*
>
> This PC has no speaker, so Tempo's captions can't hear system audio right now.
> Use this microphone for captions instead?  **[Yes] [No]**

**Yes** switches "Listen to" to Microphone, saves the choice, and restarts the
caption engine immediately. **No** leaves everything as it was. Each device arrival
asks at most once — the question is only re-armed if the device disappears again,
so there is never a nagging loop.

### The reverse prompt
If captions are running on the microphone (your explicit choice — usually because
there was no speaker) and a **speaker** gets connected, Tempo asks whether to switch
to system audio so videos and games get captioned directly. Same rules: Yes/No,
once per arrival, your call.

### Both devices? Your choice, now by name
The device line in Settings → Live Captions now names **both** devices:

> 🔊 Speaker: KJW24FVX (NVIDIA High Definition Audio)  ·  🎙 Mic: Blue Snowball

…so the "Listen to" picker right above it (Auto / System audio / Microphone) is an
informed choice, not a guess. The no-speaker warning also names the microphone that
IS available.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
