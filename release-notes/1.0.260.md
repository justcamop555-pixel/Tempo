## Tempo 1.0.260

**Tempo's own captions now read like Windows Live Captions — plus smarter audio and
better tab identification**

### One long running line
Tempo's own engine used to replace the caption bar every couple of seconds with the next
short fragment — you'd lose the sentence you were mid-way through reading. It now builds
one continuous line from those fragments, exactly like the Windows Live Captions bar:

```
♪ Powershell · Speaker 1: … behind the mountains and the river keeps
flowing gently through the quiet valley toward the distant sea
```

The newest ~140 characters stay on screen and older words shed quietly off the front.
The caption bar itself also grew — **three lines instead of two, 80% of the screen wide**
— so the longer text actually fits.

### Smarter audio system
- **Bigger hearing window:** each transcription pass now hears 2.8 seconds instead of
  2.0 — more context per pass means noticeably fewer garbled words.
- **Cuts at pauses, not mid-word:** each window now looks for a natural moment of quiet
  near its end and cuts there, handing the rest to the next pass. A word chopped in half
  can't be mis-heard as a different word anymore.
- **Wider overlap** between windows, so words on the boundary are seen whole by both.

### Better "which tab is playing" identification
- Tab titles are cleaned before use: "(3)" unread counters and 🔊 glyphs are stripped.
- Browser profile names ("Personal", "Work", "Profile 2"…) are no longer mistaken for
  the site name.
- When a **background** browser is the one making sound, Tempo scans all of that
  browser's windows and prefers the one naming a known media site — the tab actually
  playing — instead of whatever window happens to come first.
- YouTube Music, Spotify, SoundCloud and Apple Music are now recognised by their proper
  names.

Tip: the accuracy of Tempo's own captions depends most on the model — Settings → Live
Captions → Tempo model. Small hears clearly better than Base; Large Turbo is the best
and understands 90+ languages.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
