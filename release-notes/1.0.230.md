## Tempo 1.0.230

**No more ghost text when you scroll over a background image**
- In 1.0.229 the page labels and checkboxes became transparent so your wallpaper shows through them — but scrolling a tab could leave faint ghost / overlapping text behind (you'd notice it most around the Clicker tab's IDLE / "Notify when a fixed run finishes" area).
- Fixed two ways: the flat checkboxes and radios now paint the slice of the wallpaper behind them into their own buffer (so they never leave stale pixels), and scrolling now repaints the controls on the page — not just the background — so the transparent labels redraw cleanly. Verified ghost-free scrolling both up and down.

Cumulative — includes the 1.0.229 wallpaper-transparency, tray and multi-point work, the 1.0.228 redesign, and the 1.0.227 smooth-scroll fix.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed - click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
