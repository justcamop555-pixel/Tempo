## Tempo 1.0.236

**A fresh, more polished look — across every tab**

The whole UI got a redesign pass to match a cleaner, more modern card style. Because it's all built from shared components, the new look lands on **every tab** at once (Clicker, Multi-Point, Macros, Statistics, Keybinds, Settings):

- **New card headers.** Every section header is now an UPPERCASE, letter-spaced title sitting next to a rounded, accent-tinted icon badge — a clearer, more "dashboard" feel.
- **Glossier buttons.** All buttons now have a subtle top-to-bottom gradient and slightly rounder corners, giving them depth instead of a flat slab.
- **Accent primary actions.** The key actions — **New**, **Humanize** and **Set** (exact CPS) — are now filled in your theme's accent colour so they stand out, alongside the existing green **Start** and red **Stop**.

All of this respects whichever of the 30+ themes you've picked — the badges, gradients and accent buttons recolour to match, instantly, when you switch themes.

Cumulative — includes everything from 1.0.235 and earlier (CPS presets, the "Set exact CPS" box, quick goal buttons, themed macro progress bar, fuller translations).

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
