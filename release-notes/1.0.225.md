## Tempo 1.0.225

**A fresh, modern look — and no more overlapping labels**
- Tempo no longer uses the dated grey Windows controls. The checkboxes, radio buttons, combo-box drop arrows and the up/down arrows on number boxes are now flat and theme-aware: rounded accent-filled checkboxes, clean chevrons, and themed borders. They follow whichever of the 38 themes (and your custom accent) you've picked, so the whole window looks like one consistent app instead of flat cards wrapped around old 3-D widgets.
- Fixed the **Burst Settings** card on the Clicker tab, where "Clicks per burst" and "Pause (ms)" were drawn underneath their number boxes. The labels and fields now sit side by side on one aligned row.
- The new controls are sized to match the ones they replaced, so nothing shoves a long label into the control next to it.

This release also carries forward the 1.0.224 fixes (settings checkboxes now persist on close, the macro capture checkboxes are remembered, and "Unlock max speed" actually raises the speed cap).

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed - click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
