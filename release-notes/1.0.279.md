## Tempo 1.0.279

**Speaker-detection bug fixed: captions follow your default speaker · snappier updates**

### The bug: switching speakers left captions deaf
If you switched Windows' default output — headphones to monitor, monitor to
Bluetooth — while the old device was still connected, Windows fires no "device
stopped" signal. Tempo's caption engine and the speaker-voice detector therefore
kept listening to the OLD device, which had gone silent: captions just stopped
until you toggled Live Captions off and on.

**Fixed.** Tempo's device watcher now notices the default speaker changing and
automatically re-points three things at the new device:

- the caption engine's audio capture,
- the speaker-voice detector (keeping every voice it has learned so far), and
- the silent keep-alive stream that keeps loopback audio flowing.

### Speaker detection hardened
- A momentary device-query failure during a device change could flash
  "⚠ No speaker found" and trigger needless recovery for one poll. The watcher now
  holds the last known speaker through a single failed read — only two consecutive
  failures mean the speaker is really gone.
- If the audio system wasn't ready when Tempo started, the watcher now retries
  instead of staying dead for the session.

### Less delay
- **Faster caption cadence when the engine can afford it.** With 1.0.277's encoder
  shortcut, the light models transcribe far faster than real time — so captions now
  automatically switch to a ~25% shorter update step while that headroom lasts.
  Slower machines and the big models keep the stable full step; nothing gets worse.
- Internal audio-loop waits tightened (80→50 ms poll, 120→80 ms first-words build),
  shaving fixed latency off every update.

Verified live: a long spoken sentence came through verbatim and complete on the
shorter cadence, with speaker colours and the source tag intact.
