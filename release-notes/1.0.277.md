## Tempo 1.0.277

**The speech engine runs several times faster per chunk — the biggest models become usable**

### The encoder shortcut
Whisper (Tempo's offline speech engine) pads every ~3-second piece of audio to a full
30 seconds and, until now, ran its encoder over that WHOLE padded window — over 90% of
the compute was spent on padding silence. Tempo now caps the encoder context to what
the audio actually fills (with generous margin) — the same trick whisper.cpp's own
streaming mode uses — cutting inference time several-fold on **every** model.

Measured live on a real PC:

- **Large Turbo (the best model): ~36–40 seconds per chunk before — 13× slower than
  real time, hopeless, the auto-downgrade always kicked it out.** Now it transcribes
  complete, correct sentences that appear within seconds — roughly 1.5–2× of real
  time.
- Normal speech with natural pauses now captions well on Large Turbo. On a long
  uninterrupted monologue it still drifts a few seconds behind, and the automatic
  model-downgrade ladder still guards that case — nothing gets worse, everything
  gets faster.
- The smaller models (tiny/base/small/medium) gain the same speedup: snappier
  captions and more headroom for the beam-search decoding that hears better.

Quality was verified, not assumed: the base model still transcribed test sentences
near-verbatim with the reduced context, and Large Turbo's timed outputs were
verbatim.

### Opening words are protected from the phantom-word filter
The confidence gate added in 1.0.273 (drops short "words" the model itself doubted —
usually noise) could occasionally eat REAL first words right after silence, where
fragments are naturally shakier. Seen live once. Quick-start chunks now use a much
stricter bar before dropping anything, so the opening words of speech survive.
