## Tempo 1.0.281

**AI word fixer stops "repairing" names · AI face analysis sees smaller faces and reacts faster**

### The word fixer learned about names
Tempo's mishear fixer repairs words the speech engines get wrong ("seawhere" →
"sea where") using Windows' on-device spell engine. But it treated every Capitalised
word the same — and a person's NAME that happens to sit within a couple of letters of
a dictionary word ("Dani", "Zira") could get silently "repaired" into that word.

The fixer now understands sentence position:

- **Sentence start** — Capitalised is normal text, still checked and fixed
  ("Seawhere did it go" → "Sea where did it go").
- **Mid-sentence** — Capitalised means a name; it is left strictly alone.

Verified live: "…tell Matilda the analysis is finished" captioned with the name
untouched.

### Face & mouth analysis: smaller faces, faster verdicts
The visual speaker detection (which face is talking) got two upgrades:

- **Sharper eyes.** The analysis frame grew from 480 to 640 pixels wide. Faces that
  were previously too small to lip-read — people further from the camera, wide
  shots, small video windows — now clear the minimum size, and every mouth region
  carries almost twice the pixels, giving a steadier "talking" signal.
- **Faster reactions.** A newly-appeared face used to build its motion history up
  from zero, so after a scene cut the "who is talking" verdict took a second or so
  to arrive. The first measurement now seeds the history directly — verdicts land
  about a second sooner after every cut.
