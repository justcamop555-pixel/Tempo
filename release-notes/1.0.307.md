## Tempo 1.0.307

**Accuracy at the decoder itself — for BOTH engines**

### Sound-tag hallucinations banned at the source
"[Music]", "(applause)" and friends used to be *generated* by the speech model
and scrubbed off the captions afterwards — losing whatever real words the
decoder might have heard instead. Now the tokens that OPEN a bracketed
annotation are suppressed inside the decoder, at sampling time, so its
probability goes to real words.

Getting this right took an offline test harness: whisper's own internal tokens
are bracketed too ("[_BEG_]", the timestamp tokens), and a naive "starts with a
bracket" ban silently produced **zero output** on the first attempt and
hallucinated segment prefixes on the second. The shipped pattern spares the
internal tokens — verified byte-identical output to no-suppression on clean
speech, with the annotation openers gone from the reachable vocabulary. The old
post-scrubbers stay in place as a backstop.

### Stricter repetition-loop guard
Whisper's entropy check — the mechanism that catches "the the the…" and looping
phrases on noisy audio — now runs at 2.8 instead of the lax 2.4 default:
degenerate low-entropy decodes are retried at a higher temperature instead of
being shown. Retries only fire on degenerate segments, so normal speech pays
nothing.

### The GPU engine's headroom now buys accuracy, not just speed
On the Vulkan engine each chunk carries **1.15 s** of trailing context into the
next decode (vs 0.75 s), so words at chunk boundaries are heard with more of
their sentence around them. Cadence is unchanged, the overlap dedupe strips the
extra repeated words, and everything stays inside the encoder's token budget.
CPU behaviour is untouched.

### Longer repetition loops caught
The output repeat-collapser now folds up to 8-word loops (was 6) — decoder
loops on noisy audio can cycle phrases longer than the old cap caught.
