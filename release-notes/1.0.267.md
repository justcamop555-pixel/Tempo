## Tempo 1.0.267

**The face AI now reads any app or site — even behind other windows — and captions
survive audio-device changes**

### Faces, anywhere the sound comes from
The face & mouth analysis used to watch only the window in FRONT. Now it follows the
sound: the media detector already knows which app is playing audio, and the analyzer
reads faces from **that window** — any website, any app, photos or video — even while it
sits **behind** other windows. It captures through the Windows compositor, so
hardware-accelerated video (browsers, players) comes out readable, not black.

Verified live: a talking face in a background window — with a different app in front —
was detected, its mouth movement measured, and it was named the active speaker.

When the audio window is unknown or minimised, the analyzer falls back to the foreground
window exactly as before.

### Captions keep going when your audio device changes
Plugging in headphones — or a game switching the output device — used to stop Tempo's
own captions with a "turn Live Captions off and on again" message, and quietly deafened
the speaker-voice matcher. Both now **reconnect to the new default device by
themselves**: the speech engine retries up to three times per session, and the voice
matcher re-arms each session. You keep watching; the captions keep flowing.

### Smarter word repair
- **Sentence starts get fixed too.** "Seawhere did it go" is now repaired to
  "Sea where did it go" — capitalised first words used to be skipped entirely.
  ALL-CAPS tags and mixed-case names remain untouchable.
- **Stutter cleanup.** Speech engines love emitting "the the" / "and and" around chunk
  boundaries; exact immediate repeats are now dropped.

Verified: *"The the quick brownn fox. Seawhere did it it go, and and the shipss sailed"*
comes out as *"The quick brown fox. Sea where did it go, and the ships sailed"*.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
