## Tempo 1.0.263

**The face AI looks harder, and misheard words fix themselves**

### Deeper face & mouth analysis
The 1.0.262 face analysis measured how much the mouth region moved — but a head turn, a
nod or a camera pan also "moves" the mouth pixels, which could label a silent face as
the speaker. Now the mouth is measured **relative to the rest of the same face**:

- a **talking** mouth moves while the forehead and eyes stay still → strong signal;
- a **moving head / panning camera** moves everything equally → cancels to zero.

Faces too small to lip-read (a few dozen pixels) no longer contribute noise to the
verdict — they're simply excluded until the camera gets closer.

### AI repairs misheard words — in every caption source
Speech-to-text mistakes usually come out as **non-words**: "seawhere", "jumpps",
"brownn". Tempo now runs caption text through Windows' built-in on-device spell engine
(the same one every Windows text box uses — nothing downloads, nothing leaves the PC)
and repairs them under strict rules:

- only lowercase words of 4+ letters — names, acronyms and gamer slang are never touched;
- only when the fix is clearly the *same word misheard*: a tiny edit
  ("jumpps" → "jumps") or the same letters split in two ("seawhere" → "sea where");
- every decision is cached, so it costs almost nothing per caption.

Verified live:

```
in:  the quick brownn fox jumpps over the lazy dog seawhere the shipss are waiting
out: the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog sea where the ships are waiting
```

Works for both Windows Live Captions and Tempo's own offline engine.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
