## Tempo 1.0.268

**Accuracy release: quiet audio gets heard, decoding gets smarter, voices match better**

### The engine hears quiet audio now
Speech-to-text models hear QUIET audio badly — a video at low volume or a distant voice
came out garbled or missed. Tempo now lifts each soft audio chunk toward a healthy level
before transcribing it:

- capped at 8× so background noise can't be amplified into phantom words;
- already-loud audio is never touched.

Verified: a sentence spoken at volume 30 (very quiet) came out essentially word-perfect.

### Smarter decoding on the light models
Greedy decoding commits to the first word the model thinks of. **Beam search** weighs
several possible word paths and keeps the best — noticeably fewer wrong words. It costs
about twice the compute, so Tempo enables it exactly where that's free: the **Tiny, Base
and Small** models (which run several times faster than real time) on CPUs with 6 or
more threads. Medium and Large Turbo stay greedy so they keep up with live speech.

### Voices match on a third feature
Speaker matching used pitch + brightness. It now also compares each voice's **pitch
spread** — how much the voice moves around while talking. A monotone reader and an
animated talker can have the *same average pitch* and used to collide; the spread tells
them apart. The gate is deliberately loose — it only vetoes wildly different intonation —
so one person's natural variation still lands on their own profile. The standard
regression (voice A → voice B → voice A) still labels **1 → 2 → 1** correctly.
