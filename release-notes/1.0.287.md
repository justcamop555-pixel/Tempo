## Tempo 1.0.287

**Live debug tells more · errors notify you · first words land sooner**

### Live debug, deeper
The Live debug window (Settings → Data & Backup) now shows more of what matters:

> Decode: beam ON · cadence very fast ×0.6 · keep-alive playing
> Session: v1.0.287 · clicker idle · events: 0 warn / 0 error

- **Decode line** — see at a glance whether beam-search hearing is active, which
  caption cadence tier the engine has earned, and that the audio keep-alive is
  running.
- **Events counter** — how many warnings/errors this session, right in the header.
- **Colour-coded stream** — errors in red, warnings in amber, so problems jump out
  of the scroll.
- **"Problems only" toggle** — one tick shows nothing but warnings and errors.

### Errors now come to you
When something goes wrong inside Tempo, you no longer find out days later from a
log: any ERROR event raises a **tray notification** with the first line of the
problem and where to look ("Details: Settings → Data & Backup → Live debug").
Rate-limited to once per minute, so even an error storm stays polite. (Respects the
"Show tray notifications" setting.)

### First words land sooner on fast engines
On the very-fast engine tier (typically the GPU), the first-words threshold after a
silence drops from 1.2 s to 0.9 s of audio. The guards that keep opening words WHOLE
(leading-silence trim, minimum-speech wait) still apply — the engine simply gets its
first look ~0.3 s earlier. Slower setups keep the proven timing.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
