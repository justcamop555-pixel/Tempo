## Tempo 1.0.280

**Settings page fix: the Save / Check-for-updates buttons were buried**

### What was wrong
The Live Captions section of Settings gained new rows over the recent releases
(device detection, the source-tag choice), but the bottom of the page wasn't moved
down with it. For **every user**, that meant:

- **Save Settings, About…, Check for updates and Reset to defaults were hidden
  underneath the "Window & Display" card** — you could see the card, but the buttons
  behind it were unreachable.
- The portable-copy note and the privacy note **overlapped each other** and ran
  through the card, reading as garbled stacked text.

### Fixed
The whole bottom of the page is re-laid-out:

- The four buttons sit in their own clear row below the last card.
- "Last checked for updates: …" now sits beneath the buttons — it used to hide
  behind the Save button even before this bug.
- The portable-copy note and privacy note each have their own space.
- The version line closes the page at the actual bottom.

Verified on screen: every button visible and clickable, no overlapping text
anywhere on the page.

Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the app isn't code-signed — click More info → Run anyway. It's safe.
